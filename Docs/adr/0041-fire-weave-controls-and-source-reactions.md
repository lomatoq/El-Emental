## Player contract

- Hold **Shift + MMB**, scroll through thirteen levels: narrow blue-root jet, stronger spreading flood, raised orbit, protective sphere. Release the chord to stop supplying it. The default level retains power1; stream power ranges .38–2.5. Plain MMB is reserved: a short fire dash was proposed to the user, not implemented as an agreed mechanic.
- While holding that chord, a fresh LMB press launches a small fast fireball; a fresh RMB press launches a twisting drill. The field empties and rebuilds over .45 seconds after a drill. Holding a mouse button before changing context cannot reinterpret it as a new shot.
- Outside the chord, LMB draws supported burning contours or heats the aimed non-floor receiver. RMB press begins a charge, holding retains it, release launches once. Charge saturates at1.2 seconds and shares the existing Earth charge pose/camera envelope.
- Space foot flight and Shift+Space held/released ring remain. Pause, focus loss, stun, school switch, death and round reset cancel owned input; held buttons must be released before reacquiring.
- At maximum held sphere power, an additional upward wheel detent releases an expanding spherical ignition wave. The user explicitly chose this gesture, matching Earth. The held field is consumed for1.4 seconds; the wave expands from2.3 to9m over.9 seconds, checks actual line of sight, heats receivers and hits the rival once. This is a spherical effect, including above the actor, not another planar ring.
- Fire uses the existing Shift+forward surf intent for low flight: up to15m/s at approximately.55m foot clearance, requiring real support. Space or any mouse spell takes precedence. Earth surf remains unchanged. The requested visual is backward hand and foot jets plus a burning wake, using Fall legs and bounded18-degree lean.
- Held RMB grows a visible charged fireball rather than only increasing a hidden damage number. The charge profile preserves quick taps, retains full charge until release, reaches.95m visual radius and.72m collision radius, and uses a collision-checked muzzle. Final native visual approval is pending.
- Drawing keeps at most12 supported anchors, approximately .65m apart. A pointer jump does not bridge a cliff. Each anchor lasts1.35 seconds, so the oldest tail expires before the newer head; drawing alone does not create another three-second residual ground ignition. Soot/heat can fade separately. New anchors recycle the oldest at capacity, counted by RecycledSources.
- An actual fire projectile or held stream crossing an existing contour/burning receiver can cause an additional burst. A projectile amplifies once, reaction admission is at most once per .65 seconds per controller and per receiver, and bursts do not recursively create projectiles. Visibility is checked before consuming an existing receiver's reaction cooldown.

## Authority and collision

Pure FireWeaveControls owns semantic edges, mode levels and cancellation; it has no InputSystem, UnityEngine or presentation dependency. EarthInputAdapter remains the sole device reader. The local-duel capability gate remains unchanged; this is not a new replicated online school.

FireAbilityController uses eight preallocated projectile slots, retained ray/overlap buffers, generation-checked source handles, and existing FireWorldImpact destruction/heat transactions. Stream range/radius scale with sqrt(power), damage and thermal input with power. Rapid shots travel38m/s, charged bolts24–35m/s, drills48m/s. A drill only continues through a receiver if the accepted impact actually retires its blocking collider. Orbital muzzle relocation checks the real projectile radius and starting overlap before sweeping; it cannot move a large shot through a thin wall.

Orbit protects its waist band; sphere protects around the body. Incoming dynamic bodies are checked against the next physics displacement, with a bounded120m/s lookahead and512-collider query buffer. Deflection remains physical; this is not unconditional invulnerability or permission to pass through colliders. Saturation is counted and rejects that query. Heat and rival damage retain real occlusion and duel authority. The brief field depletion also suppresses its guard until recovery exceeds.2.
