# Continuous fire transport and mesh-area burning

September 10, 2026. Uncommitted on b646f675. Supersedes the three-card attached burning and four-card accent description in ADR0039; gameplay heat/damage authority is unchanged.

## Problem and research decisions

The user rejected evenly sized capsule-like fire, foot jets that stop at the floor, a short disconnected bolt tail, and a single overbright point of surface burning. Merely adding a few sprite cards did not resolve those defects.

[NVIDIA GPU Gems, Fire in the Vulcan Demo](https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems/part-i-natural-effects/chapter-6-fire-vulcan-demo) describes combining recorded animated flame/smoke sprites, varied lifecycles and careful transparency. Its moving-emitter discussion is directly relevant to disconnected particles. We retain the supplied Hovl RGBA flipbooks as visible moving detail, with ordinary alpha for smoke rather than additive smoke. Twelve hot age samples and four cooling samples replace three plus one, without increasing draws per flow. Young bright volume remains inside a narrower late volume, so opaque overlapping parcel bodies no longer dominate every outer edge.

[Unity emission documentation](https://docs.unity3d.com/6000.0/Documentation/Manual/PartSysEmissionModule.html) separates time and distance emission. Our actual defect was that each substep used the latest frame nozzle position. The solver now reconstructs timestamped births along the sampled emitter path, with a collision sweep before admission. This improves source continuity instead of masking frame batches with larger particles.

[Bridson et al., Curl-Noise for Procedural Fluid Flow](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/~rbridson/docs/bridson-siggraph2007-curlnoise.pdf) and [GPU Gems 3, Real-Time Simulation and Rendering of 3D Fluids](https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/gpugems3/part-v-physics-simulation/chapter-30-real-time-simulation-and-rendering-3d-fluids) inform the distinction between convincing transported turbulent motion and a complete pressure fluid solver. We use bounded Lagrangian particles, curl acceleration, obstacle sweeps and first-contact tangential deflection. There is no pressure projection or particle pressure coupling: this is not a Navier–Stokes implementation. A new fluid framework would need measured benefit and its own architecture decision.

[Oskar Swierad's authored fire propagation breakdown](https://realtimevfx.com/t/fire-propagation-system-shader-bp-vfx/5245) highlights the importance of spatial burning coverage and the overdraw cost of covering everything. We replace one point with a bounded actual-mesh surface patch. Heating still comes from accepted gameplay contacts; visual area sampling never spreads gameplay damage by itself.

## Implemented contracts

- `FireFlowInjection.TrackEmitterPath`: opt-in swept source interpolation with exact newborn remainder integration; discontinuities reset source history. Default hand behavior remains unchanged.
- `SurfaceResidence`: at most 0.16 seconds added once on the first collision; feet use 0.12 seconds. Hot floor gas can turn and spread before becoming smoke. Particle lifetime and query budgets remain bounded.
- Bolts retain the existing 48-particle capacity, use 60 Hz integration, 128 births/s, 0.26-second hot life, 25% projectile velocity inheritance and 0.32-second cooling samples. More developed hot trail, smaller late core, age-spaced transported atlas detail, and a stronger visible smoke envelope.
- Feet retain 96 particles and the hand material's base palette/atlas, with the requested brighter fresh core. Reduced source lights remain separate. Ground columns use elongated mostly upward gas with cooling; ring retains 24 swept 45 Hz sectors and cooling occupancy. Authored tower fire now also uses the detailed ability shape configuration and the shared Hovl layer.
- `FireSurfacePatchGeometry` projects deterministic disk probes to actual triangles, including large triangle interiors and edges. Degenerate/nonfinite triangles are rejected. `FireSurfacePatchSampler` reads visible mesh triangles, or baked posed skinned geometry, admits only the contacted side, rejects floating probes, and deduplicates anchors. At most 16 root-local anchors per eight active burning seats; 16 animated Hovl flame cards plus eight cooling wisps replace the three stacked HDR tongues. Smoke emissions cycle across the same area. Heat patches refresh on accepted contacts at bounded cadence; generation invalidation retains the existing owner guard.
- Unreadable burn meshes generate an actionable warning and no fabricated proxy-hull flame area. Scratch lists and output arrays are retained. Initial mesh/skinned scratch capacity growth is cold/contact work, not a demonstrated zero-GC guarantee.
- Active surface lamps now use intensity 0.32 and range 2.8 instead of 0.9 and 4.5. Their position averages the actual flame roots, with a smooth end fade. Atlas flame emission and retained ember/soot are separate from this lamp reduction.
