# M3 Earth Core Slice

Status: complete

## Deliverables

- Explicit-cast gesture input with fixed-count resampling and line/pull/flick recognition. Pull Rock also accepts a direct ground click after selection, with actionable HUD feedback instead of silent rejection.
- `AbilityRecipeAsset` compiled to bounded runtime recipes; the Creator Suite supports schema-versioned JSON import/export.
- Replayable `MagicCommand` with tick, stable IDs, geometry, intensity, modifiers and seed.
- Line Wall, Pull Rock and Flick Throw with bounded commands, fixed-capacity wall/fragment pools, real Rigidbody impulse, typed events, physical damage and secondary terrain edits.
- Line Wall is an art-directed rectangular stone construction with a lightly chipped silhouette and a simple box collider. It does not perform terrain CSG or trigger chunk remeshing; its 22 m safety cap permits the full visible span in the playable camera.
- Line Wall preview intentionally reveals only its ground footprint through twelve dim pebble markers. The final height and volume stay hidden until cast, matching the directed interaction rather than exposing an extrusion gizmo.
- On commit the pooled wall starts buried, rises to its final pose over 0.34 seconds with cubic ease-out, and enables collision only after 72% emergence. Short-lived dust, restrained amber accents and 72 tumbling earthen chips are emitted across the camera-facing side of the wall span, so the cast reads as displaced ground without paying for voxel remeshing.
- Line Wall hold time becomes a soft-asymptotic command intensity rather than hitting an abrupt duration plateau; commit maps it to a 1.25-10.5 m bounded wall without revealing final height during preview. Taller walls rise more slowly. During emergence two non-harmonic lateral/depth impulses, vertical resistance and visible angular kick replace the former tiny sine wobble. The Earth MVP profile now keeps an undamaged wall stable indefinitely (`automaticCrackDelaySeconds = 0`); a positive delay remains an explicit compatibility/authoring option. Impact fracture still swaps to the cached deterministic Voronoi representation with preserved world dimensions.
- RMB is a mass-aware charged magic push. A tap releases a small immediate impulse; holding approaches full force over roughly three seconds. Dynamic bodies receive a real force impulse, while a wall uses its volume-derived mass and a damped anchored response. Charge and release produce restrained camera vibration through presentation events.
- RMB now locks one explicit rock, structure fragment or wall and applies a continuous mass-aware vector field while held. Release adds a final bounded impulse; default speed caps are 32 m/s for rocks and 14 m/s for intact walls. A full field moves a standard wall several metres without allowing it to lean.
- Active wall and platform fragments share `IEarthPhysicalTarget`: grabbing a piece breaks its remaining structural bonds, pauses shrink-out and resumes the normal debris lifecycle only after release.
- Gameplay chips and thrown-rock debris remain fully dynamic while shrinking: radial gravity, momentum and collision continue until scale reaches zero. Repairable wall/platform structural pieces no longer shrink by default and keep their provenance for the reassembly path.
- Extracted Earth uses four deterministic beveled rectangular block variants with convex mesh colliders. ID-derived proportions and rotations keep the authored stone language varied without runtime mesh cooking.
- Straight ground strokes still raise walls. Curved, arched and П-shaped strokes now auto-close into a tangent-plane platform; RMB during the stroke authors 0.6-3 m height. Up to six platforms persist until a heavy impact swaps the walkable collider for irregular pooled pieces.
- Shift + Space now launches a 5-9 row, arc-density-aware moving crest. Its 96-object budget carries explicit width, height, delay, hold and crest data, and rows retreat after the crest instead of remaining as a static sparse grid.
- The crest uses staggered rows, bounded radial/lateral breakup and beveled block variants rather than repeated slabs. Event-driven radial dust, chips, sparks, ground light and camera impulse give each passing row readable weight without voxel edits.
- Re-pressing and holding Space during descent predicts the local-gravity landing point, raises a short visual cushion there and limits final downward speed to 4 m/s without reporting self-impact damage to the active ragdoll.
- Wall bases use the exact endpoint chord and solve all four lower corners against the spherical surface with authored clearance, tolerance and a 0.55 m visible-voxel margin. A sliding wall continuously follows local curvature while replay-visible footprint endpoints remain unchanged.
- Platform gestures are reprojected to one surface radius and normalized to outward winding before mesh construction. The farthest hull point determines chord burial; the platform rises from fully underground with a late collider handoff, so its cap stays stable and its sides do not invert.
- MMB creates a bounded Earth-only gravity grip at the aimed point. It pulls loose rocks and structure fragments, applies readable orbit/damping, and can overload wall/platform cohesion without grabbing the character or celestial bodies.
- MMB now owns a fixed-capacity `EarthGravityGripSession` with 48 stable ID/generation handles. A fractured wall or platform copies its active targets directly into the session without allocations or a second overlap query; captured pieces keep their mass, break remaining bonds and pause shrink until the session releases them.
- Whole-wall MMB capture subscribes to the wall fracture event and caches its `IEarthPhysicalTarget` adapters when the pool is built, so reparenting or disabling the intact collider cannot lose newly activated pieces. Legacy gravity-well profile assets whose cluster fields deserialize as zero migrate to safe 48-target spring defaults.
- Controlled rectangular Earth blocks capture a local-up hover frame and use angular damping plus restoring torque, keeping them heavy and logically oriented instead of spinning like thrown rubble.
- Movable Earth uses a scale-correct object-local triplanar frame, so texture texels follow translation and rotation while retaining metre scale. Voxel chunks share a planet-local frame across chunk boundaries. Normal, roughness/AO, macro variation and restrained contact darkening are supported.
- Space is charged Earth mobility: release raises one cached chipped pillar under the player, physically carries the Rigidbody with the rising top, then launches it along local planet up. A 1.35 second hold grows the visible pillar to 7.2 m and the launch target to 19 m/s without voxel remeshing.
- Pull Rock uses one shared `EarthExtractionGeometry` for preview and commit: three restrained wire rings show the selected underground volume, the same center/radius is subtracted from the SDF, and estimated volume times material density becomes the pooled fragment mass shown in HUD.
- The extracted fragment uses a chunky authored collision/render mesh, visibly emerges from the strained surface, then eases toward an explicit held anchor in front of the character's hands. Seven short-lived event-driven crack lines and bounded dust sell material strain without becoming terrain authority.
- Unified bending clears the old character-child anchor as soon as a world target is supplied, so an unreleased rock stays at its mouse-defined world target instead of travelling with the caster.
- Flick Throw no longer requires its cursor path to remain on the planet. A short screen-space flick produces a camera-relative release direction and a speed-derived intensity; the executor clamps velocity change to 6-18 m/s, emits `FragmentLaunchedEvent`, and applies spin plus real Rigidbody impulse.
- `EarthMagicPoseDriver` parameterizes anticipation/hold/release through root lean and both arm transforms. The pose consumes input selection and typed magic events; it never writes simulation state.
- `EarthCoreSlice.unity` and UI/VFX/audio adapters that only consume events.
- Native desktop builds place `EarthCoreSlice.unity` first, so launching the executable enters the playable slice instead of the camera-only technical bootstrap scene.
- The playable slice now uses a configurable `EarthCoreVisualStyle`: warm low-frequency stone, amber strain cues, a soft-proportioned Earth Shaper, authored diorama landmarks, atmospheric star field, layered dust/shard/pulse feedback, ACES color treatment and an in-world-focused UI Toolkit HUD.
- The opening composition now uses a deterministic scaled-space sun, phased moon, distant planet and one-pass seeded star skybox. The main light shares the visible sun direction; URP receives a depth-aware atmosphere pass plus an outside limb shell. Four pooled physical meteors coexist with a bounded decorative streak system.
- `PlanetWorldProfile` drives radius, gravity, voxel scale and budgets. The Elemental Suite rebuild action supports 12/24/48/80 m authoring ranges by capability profile and surface-shell chunk selection keeps an 80 m Native High planet within its 512-chunk budget.
- KayKit Mage is the replaceable Humanoid presentation: root-motion-free locomotion, jump/fall/land, upper-body Earth casting, additive impacts and hand IK run over the existing physical proxy. The primitive pose remains a fallback rather than the visible default.
- The mouse stays free for literal screen-space wall drawing and direct object selection. A wall drag has no hidden timing window: start on ground, drag mostly sideways at any deliberate pace, and release. `A/D` rotate the Earth Shaper around local planet up and `W/S` move forward/back along that heading.
- The presentation camera follows in `LateUpdate` from an elevated shoulder frame. Its separately smoothed focus sits ahead of the character and gains bounded extra lead with forward speed, keeping the terrain and objects in front visible.
- LMB over any loose Earth-marked Rigidbody acquires it through the same mass-aware continuous bending force used by extracted fragments. Existing-stone grab/release emits typed events and does not enqueue a voxel edit.
- M1's white ramp and 32 gravity-test cubes are disabled in the playable slice so the opening frame contains only authored Earth-Core landmarks and targets.
- The HUD exposes current ability, successful casts, readable controls and rejection language without becoming simulation authority; the generated Earth sigil is a replaceable project texture rather than baked UI artwork.
